## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the serene luxury, architectural poise, and intimate warmth of a modern Indian boutique hotel nestled in Roorkee, Uttarakhand. It bridges the heritage of traditional Indian hospitality with sleek, contemporary apartment-style living and rooftop leisure. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Character:** Aristocratic yet inviting, curated, grounded, and tranquil.
- **Target Audience:** Sophisticated business travelers visiting local academic and industrial hubs, wedding parties seeking tasteful banqueting, and leisure guests seeking weekend respites with skyline culinary dining.
- **Atmosphere:** An air of unhurried elegance. The user experience must evoke the sensorial calmness of stepping into an air-conditioned forest-toned sanctuary from the subtropical heat outside.

### Design Movement: Modern Heritage Minimalism
The aesthetic fuses **Minimalism** with **Tactile Heritage Luxury**:
- Generous ivory breathing rooms balanced by deep evergreen structures.
- Restrained application of metallic champagne highlights, avoiding flashiness in favor of whispered luxury.
- Architectural micro-lines, crisp hairline divisions, and subtle physical craftsmanship.
- Editorial layouts featuring cinematic photography paired with high-contrast, literary typography.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from the Sal and Teak forests of the Shivalik foothills, polished brass vessels, and warm plaster masonry.

### Core Roles & Rationale
- **Primary (`#173D32` — Deep Forest Green):** Anchors high-priority actions, primary brand containers, navigation bars, and bold structural blocks. Imparts grounded prestige.
- **Secondary (`#C5A46D` — Muted Champagne Gold):** Used for micro-borders, elegant accents, star ratings, active states, key metric highlights, and subtle ornamental strokes. It must never overwhelm background fills.
- **Tertiary (`#8C6D53` — Natural Teak):** Represents organic materiality; applied to subtle tags, secondary narrative captions, and tactile elements.
- **Neutral Light Surface (`#FAF6EF` — Warm Ivory):** The primary canvas across light mode, providing a warm, linen-like backdrop that relieves eye strain and repudiates clinical pure whites.
- **Deep Neutral / Surface Dark (`#0F251E` — Dark Slate Forest & `#1A1A1A` — Charcoal Black):** Reserved for dramatic nighttime lounge sections, footer surfaces, and rich overlays over photography.
- **Subtle Surface Container (`#F3EDE2` — Soft Beige):** Differentiates inner content cards, table headers, and form field backings without heavy shadow drops.

## Typography

The typography pairs the editorial nobility of **Playfair Display** with the geometric clarity and high legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display drives brand romance. Headlines should generally sit on Warm Ivory or Deep Forest Green backgrounds. Maintain tight, editorial line heights with optical kerning.
- **Body & Editorial Narratives:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides relaxed, accessible reading across apartment specifications, banquet capacities, and dining menus.
- **Labels, Badges & Overlines:** Always cast labels and metadata in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) using Plus Jakarta Sans bold weights to introduce architectural cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts should evoke the sensation of strolling through an open atrium: uncluttered, structured, and deliberate.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (12-Column Grid):** Max content width constrained to `1280px` centered within a fluid outer margin (minimum `4rem`). Column gutters sit at `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (8-Column Grid):** For viewports between `768px` and `1024px`, gutters contract to `1.25rem`, with margins of `2.5rem`.
- **Mobile (4-Column Grid):** Gutters drop to `1rem`, outer margins to `1.25rem`. Content stacks naturally into vertical rhythms.

### Rhythmic Principles
- Section headers require generous breathing space (minimum `space-xl * 1.5` vertical separation).
- Architectural hairline dividers (`1px` with `#C5A46D` at `25%` opacity or `#173D32` at `15%` opacity) should be used instead of full-bleed blocks to demarcate room specifications, suite dimensions, and banquet amenities.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than conventional drop shadows, depth is manifested through tactile layering, warm tonal contrast, and diffuse ambient glows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base Warm Ivory (`#FAF6EF`) or Deep Forest Slate (`#0F251E`).
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Tiers):** Soft Beige (`#F3EDE2`) surfaces framed by a crisp `1px` border in `#173D32` (10% opacity) or Champagne Gold (`#C5A46D`, 20% opacity). Zero drop shadow.
- **Level 2 (Floating Widgets & Room Pickers):** Off-white base (`#FFFFFF`) with a warm, diffuse ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -8px rgba(23, 61, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(23, 61, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** Backdrop blur of `12px` combined with a dark slate tint (`rgba(15, 37, 30, 0.65)`). Dialogs receive a sharp, delicate gold perimeter border (`1px solid rgba(197, 164, 109, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The geometric identity is rooted in tailored craftsmanship and structural precision. Soft corners (`roundedness: 1`, `4px` default radius) provide refined tactile finish without introducing child-like or overly casual softness.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Containers, Cards, Images & Inputs:** Subtle `4px` (`0.25rem`) to `6px` radius. Clean edges dominate to reflect architectural permanence.
- **Badges, Status Tags & Small Utility Pills:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to create an intentional counter-rhythm against architectural rectangles.
- **Buttons:** Crisp, softly eased corners (`4px`), retaining classic bespoke form factor.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Reserve Suite", "Book Banquet"):** Solid Deep Forest Green (`#173D32`) background, crisp Warm Ivory text, 4px border radius. Hover introduces a subtle gold hairline outline (`1px solid #C5A46D`) and slight luminous lift.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Explore Rooftop", "View Floorplans"):** Transparent background, `1px` border in `#173D32` (or `#C5A46D` on dark sections), font weight 600, letter-spaced label. Hover fills with `#173D32` (or `#C5A46D` on dark) and inverts text color.
- **Text Link:** Understated serif or uppercase sans link accented with an underline positioned `4px` beneath the text baseline in `#C5A46D`.

### Floating Booking Bar Widget
- Horizontal multi-input dock pinned to the hero base or floating bottom on scroll.
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF6EF` with diffuse forest green ambient shadow and a `1px` `#C5A46D` hairline accent rim.
- Segmented inputs: Check-in / Check-out, Guests, Space Selection (Apartment Suite, The SkyFall Rooftop, Banquet Hall).
- Vertical micro-dividers (`1px solid rgba(23, 61, 50, 0.12)`) separating selections.

### Cards (Suites, Events & Dining)
- **Suite Showcase Cards:** Full-bleed imagery with a gentle zoom on hover (scale `1.03`, transition duration `500ms`). Bottom container rests on `#F3EDE2` with room metrics (sq ft, occupancy, balcony view) set in uppercase labels accompanied by minimal gold icons.
- **The SkyFall Rooftop & Banquet Cards:** Dark ambient treatment (`#0F251E`) featuring high-contrast typography, champagne gold subheadings, and translucent tags (`rgba(197, 164, 109, 0.15)` fill with `#C5A46D` border).

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges (e.g., "Penthouse Apartment", "Panoramic Skyline", "Pure Veg Available"):** Pill-shaped, background `rgba(23, 61, 50, 0.06)`, text `#173D32`, `1px` border `rgba(23, 61, 50, 0.15)`. On dark surfaces: champagne fill (`rgba(197, 164, 109, 0.1)`) with gold text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#F3EDE2` (or `#FAF6EF` inside dark cards), flat with a crisp bottom or full hairline border in `#173D32` (20% opacity).
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#C5A46D` (`1.5px`) with zero loud drop-shadow rings.
- Label: Floating uppercase label in `#173D32` at 70% opacity.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Square inputs with `2px` corners or round radios. Unchecked: `1px solid rgba(23, 61, 50, 0.3)`. Checked: Solid `#173D32` fill housing a crisp `#C5A46D` gold checkmark or center pip.
