An Authentic Sanctuary on Dehradun Road

Tucked serenely within the residential enclave of Awas Vikas, just moments from Vaishali Mandap on the vital Dehradun corridor, Hotel Shyam Sagar was envisioned as a tranquil haven away from highway bustle while keeping the heart of Roorkee effortlessly within reach.

We established our doors with a profound reverence for the timeless Indian tenet of Atithi Devo Bhava. Here, boutique hospitality is neither distant nor rigid; it is a personalized embrace where every cup of spiced tea is prepared to your liking, and every suite is prepared with familial mindfulness.
