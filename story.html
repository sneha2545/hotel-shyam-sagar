Our Story

A Story of Three Generations

From a modest lodge on Dehradun Road to a refined boutique sanctuary — the journey of Hotel Shyam Sagar is one of unhurried evolution.

Where It Began

A Lodge on Dehradun Road

In 1986, the Shyam Sagar family opened a twelve-room lodge on Dehradun Road — modest, clean, and known for one thing: a cup of chai that the engineers passing through never forgot. Three decades later, that same hospitality is the soul of everything we do.

The property has grown, the suites have become residential, and the rooftop is now a destination in its own right — but the rhythm of the place hasn't changed: pour the tea, set the table, greet the guest by name.

Milestones

A Quiet Evolution

1986

The original twelve-room lodge opens on Dehradun Road. Pure-veg kitchen, attached bath, and a kettle that never went cold.

1998

A second wing is added — twenty more rooms and the first banquet hall in the family. Marriages begin to find their address here.

2012

The property rebrands as Hotel Shyam Sagar and begins its slow transformation into a boutique stay with apartment-style suites.

2019

The SkyFall Rooftop opens — an open-kitchen dining destination that has since welcomed over a hundred thousand guests.

2024

Vedanta Hall opens — pillarless, divisible, with a private pre-function terrace. The city's most considered banqueting address.

2026

The great refresh — refined interiors, a new booking experience, and the digital debut of Hotel Shyam Sagar.
