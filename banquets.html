Events & Celebrations

Moments Worth Celebrating

From intimate family gatherings to professional conferences, host life's most important moments in a setting crafted for warmth, elegance, and ease.

Banqueting with Intention

A Welcoming Stage for Every Chapter

Whether you're gathering fifty or two hundred and fifty, we approach every celebration with the same care — your menu tailored, your moments choreographed, your guests wrapped in warmth.

What We Host

Five Occasions, One Address

Ring Ceremonies

An elegant gathering for family and friends — intimate seating, floral mandap, and a pure-veg menu curated for the occasion.

Birthday Parties

Make birthdays memorable — a gathering designed around your celebration, from themed décor to a custom dessert menu.

Anniversary Celebrations

Mark another beautiful year together — private cabanas on the rooftop or an intimate family dinner in our banquet hall.

Business Conferences

Board meetings, training days, dealer meets and conferences of up to 80 delegates with AV, refreshments, and dedicated service.

The Venues

Spaces Crafted for Togetherness

Two primary venues — our pillarless banquet hall and the open-air SkyFall rooftop terrace — plus four private cabanas on the rooftop level.

Venue Vedanta Hall

Pillarless banquet hall with brass chandeliers, divisible into two, and a private pre-function terrace.

Venue The SkyFall Terrace

Open-air rooftop with string lights, cabanas, and panoramic views — reserved for weddings and milestone dinners.

Capacity 180 — 250

Theatre and floating capacity for conferences; seated banquet capacity for celebrations.

Cuisine Pure Veg

North Indian, Kumaoni, Continental, and live counters for chaat, pasta, and waffles on request.
