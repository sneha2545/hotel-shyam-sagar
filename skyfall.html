The Atmosphere

A Cabanas, String Lights & an Open Sky

The rooftop seats sixty — twenty of those in candlelit cabanas draped in raw linen, the rest under a canopy of string lights that frame the Shivalik foothills on a clear evening.

Music is curated, never loud. Service is attentive but never intrusive. The kitchen is in plain view — every kebab, every bread, every pour is part of the performance.
